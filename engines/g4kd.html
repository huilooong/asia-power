Will it fit my vehicle?

The G4KD code alone does not confirm a direct replacement. Match your vehicle model, year, transmission, mounts and electrical connections with the donor.

The linked stock includes Hyundai ix35 and Sonata donors. Donor years describe those individual listings; they are not a compatibility range for every vehicle.

What to check before buying

  • Clear photos of the engine-code marking and donor vehicle.
  • Condition photos, and a running video or test results where available.
  • Photos of mounts, connectors, harness and included accessories.
  • A written list of included parts and any visible damage.
  • Packing details and the exact basis of the export quotation.
