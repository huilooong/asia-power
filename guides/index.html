Prepare your enquiry

  1. Provide the vehicle model, year, full engine code and gearbox type.
  2. Specify a bare engine, long block, engine with accessories or a complete half-cut.
  3. Include the destination country and port, then agree the quotation and delivery scope.

Before placing an order

Is the same engine family enough to confirm compatibility?

No. Compare the complete code, donor vehicle, wiring, ECU, emissions version and transmission with your workshop.

What evidence should I request for the offered engine?

Request readable engine-code photos, the stock reference, an agreed component list and inspection or startup evidence for that specific unit.
