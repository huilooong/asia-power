Preferred Ports for East African Importers

Choosing the right port can significantly reduce shipping costs and simplify customs clearance. In East Africa, the following ports are strategic for importing engines:

  • Port of Dar es Salaam, Tanzania – A key entry point for goods heading to Tanzania and nearby regions.
  • Mombasa Port, Kenya – The largest port in East Africa, serving Uganda as well through the Northern Corridor.
  • Kampala Inland Port, Uganda – Although Uganda is landlocked, goods shipped to Mombasa can be efficiently transported to Kampala.

Clearing Customs Efficiently

Understanding the customs process is vital to ensure a smooth import experience. It's advisable to engage with reputable customs agencies, such as:

  • Tanzania Revenue Authority (TRA) for shipments to Tanzania.
  • Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) for Kenyan imports.
  • Uganda Revenue Authority (URA) for goods entering Uganda.

These agencies can aid in demographic duty determinations and expedite clearance procedures, minimizing importation delays.
